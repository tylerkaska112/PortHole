# 0152 ntdll: opt-in `env.MADEIRA_GLUED_MAIN = 1` gives the game's own process a glued PartitionAlloc pool, in two real parts

`build/ntdll-unix/virtual_ios.c` (the jumbo branch of `NtAllocateVirtualMemory`, new `ios_glued_main`). Off unless the variable is 1; the app sets it, together with `MADEIRA_SC_PA_POOLS=1`, for BeamNG.drive only (`GameFixes`, `gluedPools`).

Reason (BeamNG.drive 0.39.4.0, Steam, 2026-10-16, three device logs): after patch 0151 the game gets past its first refused free and then stops with `0x80000003 STATUS_BREAKPOINT` (its own `gameStartupError`). Chromium's PartitionAlloc, inside the game's interface, asks for 32 GB at a random 32 GB-aligned hint three times, each time freeing the answer (the soft slot at 0x7400000000, 16 GB aligned only), then asks for 64 GB - 64 KB (= 2 x 32 GB - 64 KB, to find an aligned 32 GB inside it), which no hole in the map holds (`[jumbo] kernel-pick reserve failed (0xc0000017) for size=0xfffff0000`). The same sequence killed Social Club's helper (documented at `ios_sc_glued_pools`); Madeira's fix there is `MADEIRA_SC_PA_POOLS=1`: the emulator's arena starts 4 GB higher, and the helper's 32 GB ask gets [0x7800000000,0x7d00000000) as one real view (the regular pool and the first 4 GB of the BackupRefPtr pool) while it is told it has 32 GB. That path was only taken when the process is SocialClubHelper.exe, and its single 20 GB view does not fit any more: with the arena moved (`MADEIRA_SC_PA_POOLS=1`) BeamNG's first try was `[sc-cef] glued PartitionAlloc pools: [0x7800000000,0x7d00000000) is not free (st=0xc0000018)` because the 64-bit DLL farm is mapped in [0x7a00000000,+4 GB) (`[mixed-map]` lines at 0x7a7b...). So with `MADEIRA_GLUED_MAIN=1` (new `ios_glued_main_grant`) the game's own process gets the two parts PartitionAlloc really fills as separate real views: [0x7800000000,+8 GB) (the bottom of the regular pool) and [0x7c00000000,+4 GB) (the bottom of the BackupRefPtr pool, held by the boot layout), and is told it has 32 GB at 0x7800000000. PartitionAlloc takes super pages first-fit from the bottom of each pool; a commit past either part fails as on any unreserved address. Given once per process.
The next ask (485's log, 02:06) is PartitionAlloc's metadata region: 16 GB at a random hint (`jumbo#2 ... hint=0x1beda1a60000`). The old soft-slot answer was slot-relative (`0x71a1a60000`, nothing behind it) and its first commit failed with `0xc0000019`; patch 0108 (served from the map's biggest hole, first part real) is the answer for exactly that and was only on with `pa-pool32-soft`: with `MADEIRA_GLUED_MAIN=1` it is on too.
Tried first and dropped: `pa-pool32-soft` (the Red Dead Redemption 2 layout) made it worse on this game (every 32 GB ask failed at once, no `[pa32]` line); a soft slot on a 32 GB boundary (0x7800000000) would have had PartitionAlloc's BackupRefPtr half over the emulator's arena at 0x7c00000000 (a collision at its first commit).
How to test: start BeamNG.drive from Steam; the log should show `[sc-cef] 0152 the game's own process gets ...`, `[sc-cef] env.MADEIRA_SC_PA_POOLS=1: FEX arena ...` at start and `[sc-cef] 0152 glued PartitionAlloc pool for the game's own process: 32 GB reported at 0x7800000000, really ...` at the first 32 GB ask, then no 64 GB ask (`size=0xfffff0000`). UNKNOWN: whether the game then runs; the emulator's arena is 12 GB instead of 16 GB for this game. Untested on a device.
Library: `gl-lib-0152c` (ntdll `virtual` object rebuilt on `gl-lib-0151`, which has 0151). Earlier cuts of 0152 (build 484: the helper's 20 GB view for the game too; build 485: the two real parts only) are superseded.

## Version 4 (build 487): Social Club's layout 2 for the game's own process
The 02:12 log (build 486): `jumbo#1` (the pool, two real parts) and `jumbo#2` (the 16 GB metadata, patch 0108) were granted, then `jumbo#3`-`#5` were three more 32 GB asks from a SECOND PartitionAlloc (caller `grip=0x139ec1050`; `libcef.dll`'s, as in Social Club's helper), each answered with the soft slot at 0x7400000000 and freed, then 64 GB - 64 KB twice (`kernel-pick reserve failed`) and `0x80000003` again. The map has two 32 GB boundaries outside the carveout, 0x7000000000 and 0x7800000000; the first is full of the furniture window, so one allocator can have a pool and the second cannot. Madeira's layout 2 (`MADEIRA_SC_PA_POOLS = 2`; the app puts the JIT pool's RW alias at 0x7900000000 and holds [0x7000000000,+4 GB) before Wine starts, and `ios_sc2_route` gives chrome_elf's pool, libcef's pool, their metadata regions and Oilpan's cage each a slot) was made for exactly three-allocators-in-one-process; it was only used when the image is SocialClubHelper.exe. Now `ios_sc_current_is_helper()` is also true for the game's own processes when `MADEIRA_GLUED_MAIN=1` and the layout is 2, so `ios_sc2_route`, the 2 TB limit report and the V8 sandbox cage step (`ios_sc2_ex_cage`) apply to them. The earlier two-part grant (layout 1) stays as the fallback when layout 2 cannot be set up (the app's alias did not land at 0x7900000000).
The app: the BeamNG game fix now sets `MADEIRA_SC_PA_POOLS=2`, `MADEIRA_GLUED_MAIN=1`, and adds `env.MADEIRA_SC_PA_POOLS = 2` to the game's config text (`GameFixes.Fix.configLines`, applied in `EngineLaunch.apply`), because the app reads the layout from the config, not the environment, when it places the JIT pool's alias.
How to test: the log should show `[sc-cef] layout 2: [0x7000000000,+4GB) held for libcef.dll's pools; RW alias hint 0x7900000000` at start and `[sc-cef] layout 2: ... PartitionAlloc pools for SocialClubHelper.exe: 0x7800000000` / `0x7000000000` (the text says SocialClubHelper.exe, it is the game) for the two pool asks, then no `size=0xfffff0000`. UNKNOWN: whether the game runs; layout 2 was sized for GTA V and RDR2, and BeamNG is a third Chromium host with a different order of asks. Untested on a device.
Library: `gl-lib-0152d`.
