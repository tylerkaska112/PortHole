# 0154 ntdll: the unclaimed V8 cage holdback also serves requests that only the relaxed window can place (opt-in with `env.MADEIRA_CAGE_RELEASE = 1`)

`build/ntdll-unix/virtual_ios.c` (`map_view`): after the clamped scan fails and the ceiling is relaxed, `ios_cage_release_on_exhaustion` is tried again with the relaxed range and the reserved areas are searched once more before the kernel pick. With the switch off nothing changes.

Reason (BeamNG.drive 0.39.4.0 on Ultralight, log 2026-10-16 03:11, build 493): the ICU data is found now, but Ultralight (WebCore.dll+0x181d947) then reserves 4 GB, 2 GB, 1 GB, 512 MB ... down to its 32 MB minimum, every one 4 GB-aligned, and each ends in `STATUS_NO_MEMORY`; the game's crash reporter fires and the process exits with 1. The kernel pick needs the size plus 4 GB of alignment slack (`[mmap-tag] ALL 5 TAGS FAILED size=0x180000000` for a 2 GB ask) and the largest hole in the host map is 3.9 GB, because the address space the app can use is only the top 64 GB (CoreAnimation owns the rest) and Chromium's pools, the cage and the FEX arena fill it. The 8 GB V8 cage holdback (`[cage] holdback reserved 0x7a00000000+0x1ffff0000`) is never claimed in this game (no V8), but `ios_cage_release_on_exhaustion` only looked at the clamped window, which ends at 0x7200000000, so it could never apply. A 2 GB ask can now be placed at 0x7b00000000 (the holdback's only 4 GB boundary); the 4 GB ask still fails (the holdback is 64 KB short of 4 GB) and Ultralight goes on to the next size.
How to test: the log should show `[cage] holdback handed to the allocator` and `[cage] 0154 #1 relaxed window: 0x80000000 bytes (align 0x100000000) placed at 0x7b00000000`, no `[jumbo#7]` failure, and no CrashSender.exe start. UNKNOWN: whether Ultralight works with a 2 GB cage, and what it asks for next. Untested on a device.
Library: `gl-lib-0154` (ntdll `virtual` object rebuilt on `gl-lib-0153`).
